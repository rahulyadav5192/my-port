Rentex

Rentex is a full-featured Property and Tenant Management System built to automate daily rental operations with reliable workflows and centralized controls.

Live URL: propilor.com

About Rentex

Rentex centralizes tenant operations, rent management, and maintenance tracking in one platform. It is designed for landlords, property managers, and operations teams that need accurate records and less manual effort.

The system unifies rent billing, payment visibility, tenant lifecycle, and property-wise issue tracking so teams can manage operations from a single dashboard.

Problem

Most rental workflows are still handled on spreadsheets and chat, which causes payment delays, poor follow-up, and difficult tracking across properties.

Key problems observed:

  • Rent collection and billing were manually handled and error-prone.
  • Tenant and property data lacked a single source of truth.
  • Maintenance requests were difficult to monitor end-to-end.
  • Teams had no clear dashboard for pending and completed tasks.
  • Operational reporting required extra manual reconciliation.

This created operational friction and delayed day-to-day decision making.

Solution

Rentex provides a reliable, automation-first backend that streamlines rental workflows.

How FinPulz solves the problem:

  • Automates rent collection and recurring billing workflows.
  • Provides property and tenant dashboards with actionable data.
  • Tracks maintenance requests with clear status transitions.
  • Improves accountability with role-based data visibility.
  • Supports scalable operations as properties and tenants grow.

With Rentex, rental teams reduce manual work and improve consistency across operations.

My Role

I led backend architecture and implementation with a focus on performance, data consistency, and maintainability. I:

  • Designed API structure and module boundaries for tenant/property operations.
  • Implemented rent collection and billing logic using Laravel + MySQL.
  • Built maintenance ticket workflows with reliable status tracking.
  • Added role-based access controls for secure operations.
  • Optimized database queries and reporting endpoints for speed.
  • Worked with frontend teams to deliver stable integrations.

Vision

Rentex is built to become a dependable operational layer for real-estate businesses by making day-to-day rental management structured, trackable, and scalable.
